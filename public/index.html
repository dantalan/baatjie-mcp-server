How posting works

    
One-tap share (12 platforms): the chip opens the platform with your caption already in the post box.

    Copy + open (19 platforms): the chip copies the caption and opens the platform, where you paste it and attach the card.

    Copy · post in app (2 platforms): phone-only apps. The chip copies the caption for you.


    Sign-in and upload run in the localhost edition, served by your master MCP at http://localhost:8641. There, Log in opens the platform in the sigsche browser (its own Chrome window that keeps your sessions), and each Queue chip becomes Upload ↑: it opens the post box in that signed-in window, attaches the card and fills the caption, and you press Post. Bluesky, Mastodon, Telegram and Discord can post fully automatically once you save an API token. Mxit closed years ago, so its slot now holds Google Business Profile, which is where agencies get found locally.
